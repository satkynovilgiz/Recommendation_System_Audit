01 / Research overview

Look beyond
the engagement score.

What should an auditor examine when a recommendation system is designed to maximize engagement?

This report extends Engagement as Objective into an engineering and auditing specification. It combines predicted engagement, independently estimated content quality, and a moral-emotional outrage indicator in a multi-objective contextual bandit framework.

It connects fair ranking and constrained learning with an audit protocol for examining disclosed ranking documentation and logged behavior.

04 / Interpretation

An audit has
boundaries.

Internal consistency does not settle which objective a platform should optimize.

The framework examines whether stated objectives and constraints agree with logged behavior. Its practical usefulness depends on estimator validity, subgroup coverage, suitable documentation, and continued evaluation as content producers adapt.

This website summarizes the authors’ report. The reported experiments have not been independently reproduced here; the site provides the paper, not an executable reproduction package.
