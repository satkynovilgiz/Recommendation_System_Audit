01 / Research Context

Moving from diagnosis
to prescription.

How can platforms structurally bound extremity concentration without abandoning engagement?

This technical report extends "Engagement as Objective" into a robust engineering specification[cite: 1]. It outlines a contextual multi-armed bandit (CMAB) architecture featuring independent quality and moral-emotional outrage estimators[cite: 1, 5].

By connecting fair-ranking principles with automated audit checklists, the report bridges theoretical constraints and real-world compliance[cite: 1, 2, 8].
